# ICT Incident Report

**Incident ID:** INC-2026-001

**Report Status:** Resolved

## 1. Incident Summary

**Title:** Ransomware Attack on Core Banking Platform

At 02:17 CET on 15 March 2026, the SOC detected anomalous encryption activity on the primary database servers hosting the core banking application. The LockBit 4.0 ransomware variant encrypted 340 GB of production data across the core banking database cluster before containment was achieved. Online banking services were unavailable for approximately 6 hours and 45 minutes.

## 2. Detection

**Detection Method:** SIEM alert triggered by anomalous file I/O patterns on database servers; correlated with EDR detection of known ransomware indicators on two compromised administrator workstations.

**Detection Timestamp:** 2026-03-15T02:17:00+01:00

## 3. Timeline


- 01:45 CET: Initial compromise via spear-phishing email opened by a member of the IT operations team (after hours)
- 02:17 CET: SOC receives SIEM alert for mass file encryption on DB-PROD-01
- 02:22 CET: EDR isolates two compromised workstations; SOC escalates to Incident Commander
- 02:35 CET: Decision to shut down core banking application to prevent lateral spread
- 02:45 CET: Online banking portal displays maintenance message; client-facing services offline
- 03:10 CET: Forensic analysis confirms LockBit 4.0 variant; no evidence of data exfiltration at this stage
- 05:00 CET: Backup integrity verified; restoration from immutable backups initiated
- 09:30 CET: Core banking application restored in read-only mode for critical operations
- 09:45 CET: Full read-write operations restored; online banking portal back online
- 10:00 CET: Post-incident monitoring confirms stable operation

## 4. Affected Systems

- Core banking database cluster (DB-PROD-01, DB-PROD-02)
- Online banking portal
- Internal payment processing engine
- Client reporting module

## 6. Root Cause Analysis

Spear-phishing email bypassed email gateway filtering due to a misconfigured attachment scanning rule introduced during a maintenance window on 12 March. The compromised user account had excessive privileges (domain admin) contrary to least-privilege policy.

## 7. Remediation Actions

Restored from immutable backups; rotated all privileged credentials; patched email gateway configuration; implemented additional MFA requirements for all admin accounts; engaged external forensic firm for full investigation.
