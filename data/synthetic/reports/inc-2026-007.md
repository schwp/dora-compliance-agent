## 1. Incident Summary

**Title:** Single Employee Email Account Compromised via Phishing

On 1 July 2026, a marketing team member's email account was compromised after clicking a phishing link. The attacker accessed the mailbox for approximately 2 hours before detection, sending 3 phishing emails to internal contacts. No client data was accessed — the compromised account had no access to client-facing systems or portfolio data. The account was disabled and credentials reset.

## 2. Detection

**Detection Method:** Email security gateway flagged outbound phishing emails from the compromised account; SOC investigated and confirmed account compromise.

**Detection Timestamp:** 2026-07-01T10:18:00+02:00

## 6. Root Cause Analysis

Employee clicked phishing link in an email impersonating an internal IT notification. MFA was configured but the phishing page used an adversary-in-the-middle technique to capture the session token.

## 7. Remediation Actions

Account disabled and recredentialed; phishing-resistant MFA (FIDO2) deployment accelerated; targeted security awareness training for marketing team.
