## 1. Incident Summary

**Title:** Major Cloud Provider Outage Affecting NAV Calculation Pipeline

On 22 April 2026, a region-wide outage at NimbusCloud GmbH's Frankfurt data centre rendered the fund's NAV calculation infrastructure unavailable for 4 hours and 20 minutes. The outage affected multiple compute instances and the managed Kubernetes cluster hosting the valuation engine. End-of-day NAV publication for 12 sub-funds was delayed by 3 hours, impacting 215 institutional investors across 4 EU jurisdictions.

## 2. Detection

**Detection Method:** Automated health check failure on Kubernetes pods; NimbusCloud status page confirmed regional outage at 14:05 CET.

**Detection Timestamp:** 2026-04-22T13:55:00+02:00

## 3. Timeline


- 13:55 CET: Kubernetes pod health checks begin failing across the Frankfurt region

## 6. Root Cause Analysis

Power distribution unit failure at NimbusCloud's Frankfurt data centre (FRA-DC-02). Redundant power path failed to activate due to a firmware defect in the automatic transfer switch, identified by NimbusCloud as a known issue pending remediation.

## 7. Remediation Actions

Activated manual NAV fallback procedure; NimbusCloud replaced defective transfer switches; initiated review of multi-region failover architecture; raised formal complaint with NimbusCloud under DORA Art. 30 SLA provisions.
