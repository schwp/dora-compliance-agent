## 1. Incident Summary

**Title:** DDoS Attack on Client Reporting Portal

On 3 June 2026, a volumetric DDoS attack targeted the client reporting portal, causing intermittent availability for approximately 1 hour and 45 minutes. The attack peaked at 12 Gbps and was mitigated by the CDN provider's automatic scrubbing. No data was compromised and no critical functions were affected, as the reporting portal serves read-only report downloads.

## 2. Detection

**Detection Method:** CDN provider (Cloudflare) automatic DDoS detection; alert forwarded to SOC.

**Detection Timestamp:** 2026-06-03T10:15:00+02:00

## 3. Timeline


- 10:15 CET: Traffic spike detected by CDN; automatic mitigation engaged
- 10:20 CET: SOC receives alert; confirms client portal experiencing intermittent errors
- 10:45 CET: CDN mitigation fully effective; portal responding normally
