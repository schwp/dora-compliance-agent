## 1. Incident Summary

**Title:** Third-Party Pricing Data Feed Interruption

On 17 June 2026, the real-time market data feed from a third-party pricing provider experienced a 3-hour outage due to a configuration error at the provider's side. The outage affected the entity's ability to perform real-time portfolio risk calculations. Manual pricing procedures were activated after 45 minutes. No client-facing services were disrupted, as reinsurance contract pricing is not real-time dependent.

## 2. Detection

**Detection Method:** Automated data quality check flagged stale pricing data; confirmed with provider's status page.

**Detection Timestamp:** 2026-06-17T08:30:00+02:00

## 3. Timeline


- 08:30 CET: Data quality monitor flags stale prices on 340 instruments
- 08:45 CET: IT contacts pricing provider; confirms their feed is down
- 09:15 CET: Manual pricing fallback activated using end-of-day prices from previous session
- 11:30 CET: Provider restores feed; automated reconciliation confirms no pricing errors in manual period
- 12:00 CET: Normal operations resumed

## 4. Affected Systems

- Market data feed ingestion
- Risk calculation engine

## 6. Root Cause Analysis

Provider deployed a configuration change to their feed distribution servers that inadvertently blocked the entity's IP range. Provider's change management process did not include client impact assessment.

## 7. Remediation Actions

Provider rolled back configuration; entity added secondary pricing source for critical instruments; updated third-party monitoring to include heartbeat checks every 60 seconds.
