## 1. Incident Summary

**Title:** WAN Link Failure Between Luxembourg and Geneva Offices

On 20 June 2026, the primary WAN link between the Luxembourg headquarters and the Geneva advisory office went down for 1 hour and 20 minutes due to a fibre cut by a construction company in eastern France. The Geneva office (8 advisors) temporarily lost access to the central portfolio management system. Advisors switched to mobile data connections and accessed the system via the VPN backup path within 15 minutes. No client-facing disruption occurred.

## 2. Detection

**Detection Method:** Network monitoring system flagged WAN link down; ISP confirmed fibre cut.

**Detection Timestamp:** 2026-06-20T11:00:00+02:00

## 3. Timeline


- 11:00 CET: Primary WAN link drops; network monitoring alerts
- 11:05 CET: Geneva office reports loss of access to central systems

- 11:30 CET: ISP confirms fibre cut near Belfort, France; ETA 1-2 hours
- 12:20 CET: Primary link restored; full speed access resumed

## 6. Root Cause Analysis

Construction company severed fibre optic cable during road works. ISP's redundant path was through the same physical conduit (single point of failure not previously identified).

## 7. Remediation Actions

ISP implemented diverse physical routing for redundant path; entity added secondary ISP for Geneva office.
