## 1. Incident Summary

**Title:** Former Employee Retains Access to Client Relationship Management System

On 12 April 2026, an internal audit review discovered that a relationship manager who left the bank on 28 February 2026 still had active credentials for the client relationship management (CRM) system. Log analysis confirmed the former employee accessed the CRM 14 times between 1 March and 8 April 2026, viewing records for 340 high-net-worth clients including contact details, account balances, and investment preferences. The former employee had joined a competing private bank in Luxembourg.

## 2. Detection

**Detection Method:** Internal audit review of access rights for recently departed employees.

**Detection Timestamp:** 2026-04-12T14:00:00+02:00

## 3. Timeline


- 28 February 2026: Relationship manager's last day; HR completes offboarding checklist
- 01 March 2026: Former employee first accesses CRM (offboarding did not revoke CRM access)
- 08 April 2026: Last recorded CRM access by former employee
- 12 April 2026: Internal audit flags active account during quarterly access review
- 12 April 2026, 14:00 CET: Account immediately disabled; legal team notified
- 13 April 2026: Forensic review of all access by former employee completed
- 14 April 2026: Legal sends cease-and-desist letter to former employee and their new employer

## 4. Affected Systems

- Client Relationship Management (CRM) system

## 6. Root Cause Analysis

HR offboarding process did not include CRM access revocation in the standard checklist. CRM system was not integrated with the central identity management platform (AD/Entra ID) and required manual deprovisioning.

## 7. Remediation Actions

Account disabled; CRM integrated with central identity management for automated deprovisioning; offboarding checklist updated; quarterly access reviews changed to monthly for first 3 months post-departure.
